## Description (paste into the page body)

> **Your Endgame. The snap at midnight. To the millisecond.**
>
> Avengers Player starts *Avengers: Endgame* by itself on December 31 and keeps it locked to the clock for the whole
> film, so the snap lands exactly on 12:00:00 AM. You bring your own copy of the film; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
>
> **What it does**
> - **Finds the snap in your file.** Every copy is a little different. Avengers Player finds the snap in yours and
>   works out the exact second to press play, so you don't have to trust "9:29:30 PM".
> - **Starts by itself.** Arm the countdown and walk away. The film starts on the second, full screen.
> - **Stays on the clock.** Pause for snacks, and it catches up. A crash or a power cut? It reopens and joins
>   where the clock says.
> - **The Final Countdown.** The last ten seconds tick in the clock panel, in the style of the animation you pick.
> - **Happy New Year animations.** Stark, Arc Reactor, Hot Rod, Infinity (the Thanos one), Gamma, War Machine, TVA,
>   Doom, Fireworks or Minimal, each building the words in its own way and its own lettering at midnight. Switch on
>   **Animation sound** and each one gets a sound of its own.
> - **Your midnight, recorded.** It saves the last 40 seconds before midnight as a video clip, for everyone who forgot
>   to film.
> - **Rehearse first.** A New Year's Eve simulation shows you the whole night in a few minutes, on any day.
> - **Subtitles** load and sync themselves to the voices.
>
> **How to install**
> 1. Download `Avengers-Player-Setup-1.11.0.exe` and open it.
> 2. Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. (It appears for any new
>    app that Microsoft hasn't seen many downloads of yet.)
> 3. Open Avengers Player, import your copy of the film, and run the New Year's Eve simulation once.
