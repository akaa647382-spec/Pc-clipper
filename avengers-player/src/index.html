    Event log
    
        
    
        
      
    

      
      
    
        
    The tests move the app's own clock, not your PC's: the real countdown stays armed and comes back when a test ends.
    
        
    
          
    1New Year's Eve simulation
    
          
    Exactly like the night: the app's clock is set to December 31 at the moment you pick and runs on from there. The standby screen counts down, the movie starts by itself, the clock and the date run in the corner, the snap lands on midnight, the date rolls over to January 1 and the celebration plays. No test bar, no pass or fail. Press F for fullscreen to see it as you will on the night. Pick the start time to watch: as set, the automatic one, or 9:29:30 PM (only the simulation uses it).
    
          
    
            
            
            
            
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    2Auto-start test
    
          
    Jumps to just before the start time and checks that the movie starts by itself, on the dot.
    
          
    
            
            
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    3Midnight simulation (Snap test)
    
          
    Moves the app's clock to just before midnight. Watch the snap land on 12:00:00, the date roll over to January 1 and the celebration. Afterwards the app shows the frames that were on screen around 12:00:00: if his fingers snap on another frame, one click moves the snap point there. Pick a start time to try it: the test says exactly where the snap lands with it.
    
          
    
            
            
            
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    4Slip and recovery drill
    
          
    Plays mid-movie, then knocks the film out of sync on purpose so you can practise pressing Recalibrate.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    5Late start test
    
          
    For anyone who opens the app after the movie should have started. The app's clock is put that far past the start time with nothing armed, so the real “You are late” box comes up. Press JOIN NOW, Enter or Space: the film jumps to where the clock says it should be, starts on the clock and stays locked to it. The result says how long joining took and how close to the clock it landed. The last choice joins a minute before midnight, so you watch the snap land after a late join.
    
          
    
            
            
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    6Power cut test
    
          
    For a power cut, an empty laptop battery or a PC that is switched off. Start the countdown, then press the button: the whole app is cut off at once with nothing saved on the way out, the way a power cut cuts it off (only the app: your PC stays on). After the time you pick it starts again the way Windows starts it when the PC is back on and you sign in: it loads the countdown, imports the movie by itself and puts it where the clock says, that much further on. The result shows up below.
    
          
    
            
            
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    7Crash recovery
    
          
    Start the countdown, then press the button. The app crashes for real, comes back by itself within a few seconds, imports the movie again and jumps to where the clock says the film should be.

    Snap point
    
        
    The exact moment in your file where the fingers snap. The quick way: the app plays that stretch of the film, shows it frame by frame and points at the flash of the snap. One click on the right frame sets the snap point to the millisecond.
    
        
        
    By hand: play from 10 s before, pause on the snap, step to the exact frame, then press the button.

    Start time
    
        
    When the movie starts. Automatic is set by the snap the app found in your file, so the snap lands on midnight. Any other start time moves the snap off midnight by the same amount; the Snap test shows you exactly where it lands.

    Sound language
    
        
        
    When a movie has several sound tracks (a dub and the original), this one plays, and this one is converted by “Fix the sound”. To switch for one movie only, use Sound language in the Mission tab or press A.
